Moderation that understands tone

JEV Mod removes rude, toxic and scam messages, spots griefing and bullying across a conversation, and mutes for longer each time someone reoffends. Friendly banter and trash talk are left alone.

What it catches

Every message is checked as it's posted, edits included. Clear cases are handled instantly; anything subtle is judged by Jev, an AI model that reads tone and context, in any language.

Rudeness and insults

From snarky put-downs to outright name-calling, judged by meaning rather than a word list.

Bullying

Picking on the same person over several messages. One jab between friends isn't enough.

Griefing

Planning or bragging about wrecking other players' games, raids and spam-pinging.

Scams and phishing

Fake Nitro and Steam gifts, look-alike links, "I accidentally reported you", requests for logins, account trading. The rest of a scam run is cleaned up too.

Spam

Flooding chat, posting the same message again and again across channels, and advertising other servers.

Threats and hate

Threats, slurs and telling people to harm themselves go straight to a 24 hour mute.

Fair, escalating punishments

The message is deleted, the member gets a DM explaining why, and they're muted. How long depends on how serious it was and how many recent offenses they have.

2 min→ 10 min→ 1 hour→ 6 hours→ 24 hours, final warning→ Ban

Each repeat moves one step up the ladder. Offenses cool down: after 24 hours without trouble a member starts again at the bottom (severe offenses count for 30 days). Staff and the server owner are never moderated.

Your server, your rules

On top of the built-in rules, each server can add up to 15 of its own, in plain words. The AI judges what a message means, so it catches rephrasings without flagging innocent mentions.

Setup in two minutes

  1. Add JEV Mod to your server.
  2. In Server Settings → Roles, drag the JEV Mod role above your member roles. Discord won't let it mute anyone whose role is higher.
  3. Run /modlog and pick a staff-only channel, so your team can see every action. Give JEV Mod access to that channel.
  4. Optionally, add your server's own rules with /rule add.

Questions

Does it work in other languages?

Yes. Messages are judged by meaning, so insults and scams are caught in any language your members use.

Will it punish friendly banter?

It's tuned to leave jokes between friends, gaming trash talk and venting alone, and only acts when it's confident.

What if it gets one wrong?

Staff can see every action in the mod log and undo it with /pardon. Discord timeouts can also be lifted by hand.

What happens to our messages?

Messages are checked and not kept, apart from short excerpts in offense records. See the privacy policy.
