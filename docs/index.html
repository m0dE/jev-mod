What it catches

Clear cases are handled instantly by built-in checks. Anything subtle is judged by Jev, TypeSafe's decision model, which reads tone and the conversation around a message, in any language.

Rudeness and insults

From snarky put-downs to outright name-calling, judged by meaning rather than a word list.

Bullying

Picking on the same person over several messages. One jab between friends isn't enough.

Griefing

Planning or bragging about wrecking other players' games, raids and mass-mentions.

Scams and phishing

Fake Nitro and Steam gifts, look-alike links, "I accidentally reported you", requests for logins, account trading. The rest of a scam run is cleaned up too.

Spam

Flooding chat and posting the same message again and again; the earlier copies are removed as well. Advertising counts too.

Threats and hate

Threats, slurs and telling people to harm themselves go straight to a 24 hour mute and a final warning.

How it decides

Each message goes through cheap checks first, so the AI only sees the ones that need judgement.

Spam and keyword checks

Flooding, repeated messages, scam patterns, threats, obvious insults and mass-mentions are caught instantly, l33t-speak included. Free and unlimited on every plan.

Skip the obvious

Emoji, "gg", "lol", bot commands and text judged clean in the last hour don't need the AI.

Jev judges the rest

Jev reads the message, what it replies to and the last few messages in the room, and checks it against the built-in rules and your own. It only acts when it's confident.

Patterns, not one-offs

A message that looks like griefing or bullying is only noted for staff. Jef Bot acts when the person's recent messages show a real pattern.

Fair, escalating punishments

The message is removed and the sender muted. How long depends on how serious it was and how many recent offenses they have.

Questions

Does it work in other languages?

Yes. Jev judges messages by meaning, so insults and scams are caught in any language your players use.

Will it punish friendly banter?

It's tuned to leave jokes between friends, gaming trash talk and venting alone, and only acts when it's confident.

What if it gets one wrong?

Every action is in your app's history. Staff can undo strikes with a pardon, from Discord, the dashboard or the API.

What happens to our messages?

Clean messages are never stored. Flagged ones are kept as short excerpts in your history. See the privacy policy.

What happens when I run out of AI checks?

Nothing breaks: your apps keep running on the free spam, scam and keyword checks until the allowance resets (the next UTC day if you used the day's share, a tenth of the month's, or the 1st of next month), or until you upgrade.

Can I self-host it?

Yes. Jef Bot is open source; see the repository for setup.
