Rules and punishments

What Jef Bot moderates, how it decides, and how punishments escalate. The same rules and ladder apply to Discord servers and API apps.

On this page

Built-in rules

Every app gets these rules. The AI judges messages against them, plus your custom rules:

  1. Be respectful. No insults, name-calling, or personal attacks.
  2. No toxicity: no harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats, or telling people to harm themselves.
  3. No griefing: don't organise or brag about wrecking other players' builds, games, or experience; no raiding, spam-pinging, or flooding chat.
  4. Keep it positive. Don't trash-talk the server, its members, or its staff, or try to stir up drama.
  5. No rudeness: no hostile, demeaning, or deliberately inflammatory messages.
  6. No spam or scams: no advertising, repeated messages, fake giveaways, or phishing links.

Jev is told to leave friendly banter, gaming trash talk, normal venting and civil disagreement alone, and to let a message through when it's unsure.

How a message is judged

  1. Spam checks look at the sender's recent messages: flooding and repeating the same message. Free.
  2. Keyword and scam checks catch the obvious cases: scam patterns and fake Discord/Steam links, threats, "kys", direct insults ("you're an idiot"), hostile language, "let's grief their base", "this server is trash", and on Discord, mass-mentions. They see through l33t-speak and s.p.a.c.e.d letters. Free.
  3. Trivial messages skip the AI: emoji only, "gg", "lol", "ok", "ty", bot commands like !rank. So does text already judged clean in the last hour.
  4. Jev judges everything else (one AI check). It reads the message, what it replies to, and the last few messages in the room. Messages are bundled for up to about 2 seconds into a quick "could this break a rule?" check; only the possible problems get the full check. Jev only flags a message when it's confident (at least 90% sure for the built-in rules, 70% for your custom rules).

The result's source says which step decided: spam, rules, jev (or ai on self-hosted servers using Claude).

Manual warnings (/warn, POST …/warn) use the same ladder and add a strike too. If a user breaks the rules again within 10 seconds of a punishment (typically a flood), the new message is removed (action.type: "delete") but they aren't punished twice.

Cooldowns

Each strike counts on its own for 24 hours (30 days for high severity), then stops counting. A day without trouble puts most people back at the bottom of the ladder. Staff can remove strikes early with a pardon. Expired strike records are deleted 30 days after they stop counting.

Griefing and bullying need a pattern

One message that looks like griefing or bullying could be a joke between friends, so it's only noted: the result has "watch": "griefing" or "watch": "harassment", a watch event goes into history, and on Discord a "Watching" note goes to the mod log. No strike, no punishment.

When a later message from the same person fits the same pattern within 30 minutes, Jev looks at their recent messages together (up to 15); that check uses one more AI check. If they show a real pattern (a plan to wreck someone's base, repeatedly picking on the same person), Jef Bot acts at medium severity (high if the message itself was severe). If the message also broke another rule, such as one of your custom rules, that rule still applies straight away.

Clear insults, threats and slurs don't need a pattern; they're acted on immediately.

Spam and scams

  • Flooding: 6 messages within 10 seconds. Low severity. The earlier messages of the burst are in deleteMessages.
  • Repeating: the same message 3 times within a minute (5 times for short ones under 10 characters without a link, like "gg"). Medium. The earlier copies are in deleteMessages, from any room.
  • Advertising (judged by Jev): low.
  • Scams: fake Nitro/Steam/crypto giveaways, look-alike Discord or Steam links, "I accidentally reported you", asking for logins, selling accounts. High (a 24 hour mute and final warning), and the sender's other recent scam messages are in deleteMessages. Messages Jev thinks are likely scams, but isn't sure about, are "Possible scam" at medium. Hacked accounts are the usual culprits, so a scam isn't an instant ban. Messages warning about a scam are fine.

Custom rules

Add rules for your own community in plain words, each with a severity (default low). In the dashboard, with /rule add on Discord, or POST /v1/apps/{appId}/rules. Up to 300 characters each; 3, 15 or 50 per app by plan.

Rule: No asking for old Braains, or talking about how you miss old Braains low
REMOVEDi miss the old braains
REMOVEDcan the devs add an option to play old braains?
FINEwhat is the oldest map in braains?
  • A message that breaks one gets category: "custom" and reason: "Server rule: <your rule>", with the rule's severity, and is acted on immediately (no pattern needed).
  • Describe the behaviour, not keywords: "No sharing personal details like addresses or phone numbers" works better than a word list.
  • Custom rules are judged by the AI. They don't apply when the AI is skipped: trivial messages, AI checks used up, or a self-hosted server without an AI key.
  • If an app has more rules than the plan allows (after a downgrade), only the oldest ones up to the limit apply.

When AI checks run out

Each plan includes a number of AI checks per calendar month (UTC), shared by all apps on the account: 2,000 on Free, 30,000 on Starter, 200,000 on Pro. At most a tenth of that (200, 3,000 or 20,000) can be used in one UTC day, so a spam wave or a busy day can't use up the whole month. Once the day's or the month's checks are used up:

  • moderation carries on with the free spam, scam and keyword checks;
  • messages that would have gone to the AI are allowed unless those checks catch them, and their results include "aiSkipped": "quota";
  • custom rules and subtle rudeness aren't caught until the next UTC day (daily share) or the 1st of next month (monthly allowance), or until you upgrade (the new limits apply straight away).

Check usage in the dashboard, with /jef status on Discord, or GET /v1/account.
