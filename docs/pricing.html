What counts as an AI check?

Only messages that reach the AI (Jev) count. Most chat never does.

Free, never counted

Spam (flooding, repeated messages), scam patterns and the built-in keyword rules run first, on every message, on every plan. A message they catch is handled without the AI.

Skipped, never counted

Trivial messages (emoji, "gg", "lol", "ok", bot commands like !rank) and text already judged clean in the last hour don't go to the AI.

Counted

Everything else is one AI check, whatever the verdict. Confirming a suspected griefing or bullying pattern is one more. Checks are counted per account, across all its apps, per calendar month (UTC), and at most a tenth of the monthly allowance can be used in one UTC day.

When you run out

Nothing stops working. Your apps run on the free spam, scam and keyword checks only, and custom rules (which need the AI) pause, until the next UTC day if you hit the daily share, or the 1st of next month if the monthly allowance is used up. API responses say "aiSkipped": "quota". Upgrading raises the limits straight away.

Billing questions

How do I upgrade or cancel?

From your account page. Changing plan, updating your card, downloading invoices and cancelling all happen in the Stripe customer portal. A cancelled plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

What if a payment fails?

Stripe retries it. You keep your plan while it does; if it can't be collected, the account moves to the Free plan.

What happens to my apps if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted straight away, but the smaller plan's limits apply: only your oldest apps up to the plan's count are moderated (the rest are paused, and their Discord servers ignored), only each app's oldest custom rules up to the limit apply, newer account keys past the limit stop working, webhooks stop on Free, and history older than the plan keeps is removed. Delete what you don't need, or upgrade again, and everything resumes.
