Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Jef Bot is a hosted chat-moderation service for Discord servers, games and other apps. This policy explains what Jef Bot collects, what it keeps and for how long, who it shares data with, and how to get data deleted. "We" means the people who run Jef Bot; "customers" are the people who sign up and use it to moderate their communities; "members" and "players" are the people whose messages are moderated.

Customers: your account

  • Google sign-in. When you sign in with Google we receive and store your email address, your name and your Google account ID. We don't receive your Google password.
  • Sessions. A signed-in browser holds a session cookie (jef_session, up to 30 days) and, during sign-in, a short-lived cookie (10 minutes) that protects the sign-in flow. They're needed for the dashboard to work; we don't use advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Apps and settings. Your apps' names, custom rules, settings (such as a Discord mod log channel), webhook URLs and secrets, and linked Discord server IDs.
  • API keys. Only a one-way hash of each key is stored, with its name, a short prefix and when it was last used.
  • Usage. Per account and month: how many messages were moderated and how many AI checks were used.
  • Billing. Payments are handled by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer ID, your subscription's ID, status and renewal date, and your plan. Card details go to Stripe directly; we never see or store them. Stripe processes payment data under its own privacy policy.

Members and players: moderated messages

Customers decide which chats Jef Bot moderates. For Discord, the Jef Bot bot reads new and edited messages in linked servers (except from bots, the server owner, people with the Manage Messages permission, and roles and channels the server exempts). For games and other apps, the customer's server sends messages to our API. For each message we use:

  • the message text, and the message it replies to, if any;
  • the sender's user ID and display name (on Discord, their Discord user ID and username);
  • a few earlier messages in the same channel or room, with their senders' names, so the message is judged in context;
  • the sender's own messages from the last 30 minutes, to spot spam and patterns such as bullying.

What is sent to other companies

To decide whether a message breaks the rules, Jef Bot sends the information above, along with the community's rules, to TypeSafe AI, which runs the Jev model. If the service is configured to use Anthropic's Claude instead, it is sent to Anthropic. These providers process it under their own terms and privacy policies.

Not every message is sent: messages caught by the built-in spam, scam and keyword checks, very short or simple messages (such as "gg" or emoji), messages judged clean within the last hour, and messages checked after a customer's monthly AI allowance is used up are handled without the AI provider.

If a customer sets up a webhook, details of each moderation action, including the full message text, are sent to the address the customer chose. We don't sell data, share it with anyone else, or use it for advertising.

  • Deleting an app deletes its rules, keys, strikes and history.
  • Removing Jef Bot from a Discord server deletes that server's strikes and history and unlinks it from its app (if it happens while the service is offline, when it next starts).
  • On Discord, when Jef Bot acts it DMs the member the reason and punishment, posts a short notice in the channel that deletes itself after 15 seconds, and, if the server set a mod log channel, posts the details there including the message text (up to 1,000 characters). Customers' responsibilities

Customers decide what to moderate and are responsible for telling their members or players that their chat is moderated by an automated service, and for having a lawful basis to do so. For the messages they send us, we act on their behalf.

Your choices

  • Customers can see, export through the API, and delete their apps, rules, strikes and history at any time from the dashboard or the API.
  • Discord server staff can see a member's records with /strikes and delete them with /pardon, and remove the bot at any time.
  • Members and players should first contact the community that moderates them. You can also ask us what we hold about you, or to delete it, or ask us to delete your customer account, by opening an issue. Please don't post personal details there; say you'd like to be contacted privately.

Security

Connections to the service are encrypted (HTTPS). API keys and session tokens are stored only as hashes. Webhooks are signed so customers can check they came from us.

Children

Jef Bot is a tool for community operators, and customer accounts are for adults. Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or older where local law requires it). Game developers who moderate chat for younger players are responsible for any consent their local law requires.
