The service

JEV Mod automatically checks messages in the servers it's added to, deletes the ones it judges to break the built-in rules or the server's own rules, and mutes or bans the authors as described on the homepage. It is offered free of charge, and features may change or be discontinued at any time.

It can make mistakes

Automated moderation is imperfect. JEV Mod may miss messages that break the rules, and may act on messages that don't. It is a tool to help a server's staff, not a replacement for them. Server staff are responsible for reviewing its actions in the mod log and reversing wrong ones, for example with /pardon or by lifting a timeout.
